About

Muzik Engine Band (MEB) is a high-energy live band bringing music to the top of the dance floor at weddings, private parties and corporate events. With a full lineup of vocalists and musicians, MEB delivers a polished, professional show built around crowd favorites that get everyone dancing.

Services

MEB performs for all kinds of events with a repertoire built to fit the room.

  • Weddings

    Ceremony, cocktail hour and full reception sets — from first dance to last call.

  • Private Events

    Anniversaries, birthdays and celebrations with a live band that fills the dance floor.

  • Corporate & Galas

    Polished, professional live music for company parties, galas and fundraisers.
